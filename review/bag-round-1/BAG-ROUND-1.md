with the roast date as hour zero. The packaging and the site's counter are the same idea.
This resolves `master.md`'s `ASSUMED: business name not supplied`.

## Open rulings — NOT resolved

1. **Which bag is the site's hero.** Two plates are picked; the GLB can only be one.
   PART 2's filmed bag, PART 3's GLB and PART 4's ripping bag must all be the same object.
2. **Ember as brand colour vs device colour.** `master.md` reserves `#C4491F` for the
   counter and the CTA. C wears it edge-to-edge. Adopting C would make ember a brand
   colour and cost the counter its status as the only warm thing on a cold page.
   Declining C keeps the signed reading. Not decided.
3. **Off-white vs the signed ground.** B inverts the site's `#0B0B0C`. It may belong as a
   theme-inversion payoff further down the page (the lightweight.info device already
   logged in `REFERENCE-lightweight.md`) rather than as the hero bag.
4. **The struck date is degenerate.** On `A-white-ground-4k.png` the struck date equals
   the roast date, which asserts nothing. On the GLB the label is real type and this is
   free to fix at export. See `SCROLL-STRUCTURE.md` open decision 6.

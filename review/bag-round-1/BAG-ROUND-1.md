## Reconciled against AMENDMENT 2 — 2026-09-15, after the fact

These plates were picked while PART 1's staging was still believed locked at take 4
(pitch-black room). A parallel session reopened it (outdoors, hard sun) and wrote
`SCROLL-STRUCTURE.md` AMENDMENT 2. What that does to this round:

1. **The lighting reference did NOT move.** PART 2's revised prompt specifies the bag
   under light that is "soft, even and shadowless", with "no sunlight... no glare", and
   take 5's final frame carries no visible sun. The hard sun belongs to PART 1 only and
   is gone by ~7s. `A-white-ground-4k.png` — bright, even, shadowless — is therefore a
   BETTER match to PART 2's bag than the original near-black plate, not a worse one.

   ground ("nothing in frame but the bag and the ground it stands on"). These plates use
   studio seamless. Use them for the bag's form, finish, valve and label; do not take the
   ground or the backdrop from them. For the GLB the ground is the page, so this is moot.

   not-reopened list as matte black, and PART 2's prompt names a matte black pouch
   explicitly. B cannot become the hero bag without amending a line that was just
   reaffirmed. Open ruling 1 is therefore narrower than it looks: A is the hero unless

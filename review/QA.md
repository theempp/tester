Scope: local dry-run preview. No deployment, paid generation, purchase, or external publishing.

## Passed

- Next production build and strict TypeScript check.
- Isolated headless Chrome, desktop 1920×1080 and phone 393×852; no ordinary runtime errors or horizontal overflow; exactly one h1 and one stamp section.
- One persistent WebGL Canvas during the animated experience. Reduced motion: zero Canvas, six roast posters, a static bag, full readable DOM.
- Desktop and phone media variants checked. Film files were not re-encoded; seeks quantize to 24 fps and skip while a seek is already in flight.
- Forward/reverse checkpoints at .34, .439, .441, .60, .80, .96 and back to .10. Counter returns to the same value and the two media layers follow the same progress source.
- Single-frame film/3D swap. 1920×1080 before/after screenshots: `final-0.4399.png` and `final-0.4401.png`. Unobscured bag crop (760,325)-(1160,870): mean absolute RGB difference 0.57/255. This is a measured close match, not a claim of pixel identity across every browser/GPU.
- Local Draco GLB is about 347 KB, below the 8 MB budget. Editable Blender source, script, named stations, separate bottom seam, individual instanced beans, corrected clean-face typography and static derivative are present.
- 4× CPU-throttled fallback checks: usable page on failed film requests; static bag after failed GLB request; keyboard first focus reaches “Skip to the coffee.” The intentional model-failure test logs the expected caught load error.
- Question disclosures open. Date magnifier includes the DOM date, and the supplied stale date is covered. The original hero counter docks and persists at 48h below the hero.
